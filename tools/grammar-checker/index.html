About the Grammar Checker

This tool helps you improve your writing by identifying common grammar mistakes, spelling errors, and stylistic issues. Unlike cloud-based services, this tool runs entirely in your browser, ensuring your text remains private and secure.

Features

  • Grammar & Spelling: Detects common errors and typos.
  • Style Suggestions: Highlights passive voice, long sentences, and complex words.
  • Readability Analysis: Calculates scores like Flesch-Kincaid to help you gauge the complexity of your text.
  • Privacy Focused: No data is sent to servers; everything is processed locally.
