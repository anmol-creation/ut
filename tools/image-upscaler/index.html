How to use Image Upscale Tool?

1. Upload Image: Drag and drop your image or click "Select Image".

2. Select Scale: Choose how much you want to enlarge the image (2x, 4x, 8x).

3. Enhance: Check "Sharpen" to add clarity or "Denoise" to smooth out grain.

4. Upscale: Click the "Upscale Image" button and wait for processing.

5. Download: Once satisfied, download the high-resolution image in your preferred format.
